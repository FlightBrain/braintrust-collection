# Coworker wallet-request template

Use this as a starting point for the message you send to coworkers. **Do not send anyone yet.** First clear legal and brand approvals.

Suggested copy:

```
Hey, I'm putting together a small NFT collectible for the team. It's free,
optional, and not an investment. If you want one, here's what I need from
you:

1. Your **public wallet address only**. It starts with `0x`.
2. Nothing else. No seed phrase, no private key, no recovery phrase.
   Never share those with anyone, including me.

If you don't have a wallet, install MetaMask (https://metamask.io) or
Rainbow (https://rainbow.me) and copy the public address from the top of
the app. Save the seed phrase somewhere private offline; if you lose it,
you lose the wallet.

The drop will be on Base. Minting is free, but on mainnet you'll still
pay a tiny gas fee (a few cents). Your card will arrive in your wallet
automatically once you mint.

Totally fine to skip this. No pressure.
```

## What to never ask for

- Seed phrase / recovery phrase / mnemonic
- Private key
- Mobile wallet PIN
- Anything that grants control of the wallet

## Why the wallet address alone is safe to share

A public wallet address only lets others **send you tokens**. It does not give them control. It is the equivalent of giving someone your email address: they can send you things but not read your inbox.

## What to do if a coworker accidentally shares their seed phrase

1. Tell them their wallet is now compromised.
2. They should create a brand new wallet immediately.
3. Move any funds out of the old wallet to the new one.
4. Never reuse the old wallet.
